HYPOTHESIS TEST RESULTS
Statistical Business Analysis

Significance Level (alpha): 0.05


======================================================================
1. ONE-SAMPLE T-TEST
======================================================================

Test:
One-sample t-test

Benchmark:
97,955.500

Test Statistic:
t = 2.565

p-value:
0.012

Alpha:
0.050

Decision:
Reject H0

Interpretation:
Since the p-value (0.012) is less than the significance level (0.05),
the null hypothesis is rejected. The result provides statistically
significant evidence that the population mean of Total_Sales differs
from the benchmark value of 97,955.500.

Null Hypothesis (H0):
The population mean of Total_Sales is equal to 97,955.500.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
The population mean of Total_Sales is different from 97,955.500.


======================================================================
2. INDEPENDENT-SAMPLES WELCH T-TEST
======================================================================

Test:
Independent-samples Welch t-test

Group 1:
North

Group 2:
South

Mean - North:
142,272.679

Mean - South:
138,438.963

t-statistic:
t = 0.130

p-value:
0.897

Alpha:
0.050

Decision:
Fail to reject H0

Interpretation:
Since the p-value (0.897) is greater than the significance level
(0.05), the null hypothesis is not rejected. The result does not
provide sufficient statistical evidence of a difference between the
mean Total_Sales of the North and South groups at the 5% significance
level.

Null Hypothesis (H0):
The mean Total_Sales of the North and South groups are equal.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
The mean Total_Sales of the North and South groups are different.


======================================================================
3. ONE-WAY ANOVA
======================================================================

Test:
One-way ANOVA

Groups:
East, North, South, West

F-statistic:
F = 2.164

p-value:
0.097

Alpha:
0.050

Decision:
Fail to reject H0

Interpretation:
Since the p-value (0.097) is greater than the significance level
(0.05), the null hypothesis is not rejected. The result does not
provide sufficient statistical evidence of a difference in mean
Total_Sales across the four regions at the 5% significance level.

Null Hypothesis (H0):
The mean Total_Sales is equal across the four regions.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
At least one regional mean Total_Sales differs from the others.
